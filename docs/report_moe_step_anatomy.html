Proposed improvement plan

Ordered by confidence and effort so far, not by raw time saved — each item is a next investigation, not a guaranteed fix.

01

Extend today's playbook to the sibling dense gathers

high confidence

ragged_gather_reduce_v2.py:117, dense_gather_reduce.py:256, and ragged_scatter.py:390,403 are the same shape of question ragged_gather_v2 already answered: diagnostic hook → real-traffic capture → sweep against dense fallback → threshold fix. ragged_gather_reduce_v2 is the strongest candidate first — its existing 60%-VMEM heuristic is already a known suspect from an earlier investigation.

Mechanical, bounded, reuses a harness already built.

02

Check the near-degenerate all-reduce for a free win

high confidence, low effort

linear.py:119,214 may be all-reducing over a replica group of size 1 (TP collapses to 1 in this dp_attention config, sharding.py:236) — a no-op that still pays real collective-dispatch overhead. Verify the actual replica-group size in the HLO first; if genuinely 1, either confirm XLA already elides it, or add an explicit if axis_size(in_axis) > 1 guard in sharded_matmul to skip the call.

03

Investigate a fixed-overhead-at-small-batch hypothesis for the routing all-gather

medium confidence

all_gather_topk_indices_and_weights() (fused_moe_gmm.py:63-89) can't be deleted — it's required for correctness — but its decode-step share (11.2% of a 7.57ms step, tiny payload) versus prefill's (8.0% of 415ms, large payload) has the same shape as the SparseCore fixed-cost story. Microbenchmark it in isolation across batch sizes to check if the floor is genuinely size-independent; if so, look at overlapping it with the previous layer's independent compute, or batching several layers' routing all-gathers into one call.

Least certain of the four — needs the investigation before any fix is designable.

04

Overlap or replace the MoE combine step's collectives

open, largest effort

reduce_scatter is the single biggest non-attention prefill category at 32.0ms. Two angles: benchmark the existing hier_rs_sc.hierarchical_reduce_scatter_local custom-kernel path (fused_moe_gmm.py:322, behind ENABLE_RS_KERNEL) directly against the default psum_scatter rather than assuming it's better; or pursue compute/communication overlap across layers (start layer N+1's expert matmul while layer N's reduce_scatter is in flight) — a real pipelining design project, correctness-sensitive, scoped separately from a benchmark-and-flag fix.
